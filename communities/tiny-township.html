About Tiny Township

Tiny Township represents the best of rural Ontario living, combining peaceful natural surroundings with convenient access to urban amenities. Located on the shores of Georgian Bay, this picturesque township offers families and nature enthusiasts a perfect retreat where you can enjoy acreage, privacy, and a strong sense of community.

Why Live in Tiny Township?

  • Scenic Natural Beauty: Gorgeous Georgian Bay shoreline and rolling countryside
  • Family-Friendly: Safe, tight-knit community perfect for raising families
  • Privacy & Space: Larger properties and acreage for those seeking breathing room
  • Nature Access: Direct access to outdoor recreation and natural attractions
  • Agricultural Heritage: Connection to farming and rural traditions
  • Peace & Quiet: Escape from urban hustle without sacrificing conveniences
  • Community Values: Strong emphasis on family, tradition, and neighborly connections

Real Estate in Tiny Township

Tiny Township's real estate market is characterized by larger properties, country homes, and waterfront estates. If you're looking for space, privacy, and a connection to nature, Tiny Township offers excellent value with a variety of options from modest rural homes to luxury waterfront properties.

Property Types

  • Acreage properties and hobby farms
  • Waterfront estates and cottages
  • Rural homes with character and history
  • New construction on spacious lots

Community Features

Georgian Bay Waterfront: Beautiful shores offering boating, fishing, and water recreation.

County Road Network: Scenic drives through picturesque countryside.

Local Amenities: Small local shops, schools, and services serving the community.

Outdoor Recreation: Hiking, fishing, boating, and nature exploration opportunities.

Farmer's Markets: Seasonal markets featuring local produce and crafts.

Perfect For

Tiny Township is ideal for families seeking a wholesome, peaceful lifestyle; retirees looking to escape the city; hobby farmers and those with agricultural interests; and anyone who values privacy, natural surroundings, and community connections over urban conveniences.

Getting Around

While Tiny Township is rural and peaceful, it's still conveniently located within 20-30 minutes of larger centers like Midland and Penetanguishene, making it easy to access services and entertainment when needed.
